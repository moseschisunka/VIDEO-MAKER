# PR-1029 — Enforce strict image and stock-media controls

Status: `IMPLEMENTED` locally; supported CI verification remains required.

## Finding

The image selector and stock-footage acquisition tools declared boolean media
and production controls but accepted Python-truthy values. Strings such as
`"false"` and integers such as `1` could therefore change validation,
provenance, thumbnail, or reuse behavior. `ImageSelector` also discovered and
ranked providers before validating those controls. Direct clip search accepted
non-finite, non-positive, boolean, and non-numeric timeout values.

## Remediation

- Parse `sample_required`, `batch`, `strict_media_validation`, and
  `production_mode` before image provider discovery or ranking.
- Reject malformed image policy controls before a provider executor can run,
  including rank-only requests.
- Parse stock-media `extract_thumbnails`, `skip_existing`,
  `strict_media_validation`, `production_mode`, and `require_provenance` with
  the shared strict-boolean contract.
- Reject direct-search timeouts that are boolean, non-numeric, non-finite, or
  not strictly positive before creating output directories or querying a
  source.
