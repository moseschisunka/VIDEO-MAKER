# PR-501 — Persist voice selection across artifacts

Status: **VERIFIED**

Proposal, script, voice-plan, asset-manifest, render-report, decision-log, and
final-review schemas now accept the canonical voice identity. Manifest stage
submission validates identities across prior and current artifacts and fails on
provider/model/voice/locale/settings drift. The internal runner persists the
identity in `project_config.json`, proposal, script, voice plan, assets, and
render report.

Evidence: `tests/contracts/test_phase5_voice.py::test_voice_propagation_detects_provider_or_voice_drift` and `tests/contracts/test_phase5_gate.py::test_phase5_gate_identity_approval_segments_resume_assembly_and_transcript`.
