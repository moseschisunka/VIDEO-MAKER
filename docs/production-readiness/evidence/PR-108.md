## Implemented contract

`lib.work_order` now provides atomic, manifest-derived claim, resume,
heartbeat, release, and advance operations. Claims use a bounded lease with a
cross-thread/process lock, a monotonically increasing lease version, explicit
ownership conflicts, expiry-based reclaim, and an observable `running` stage.
The next stage is always derived from the first non-completed manifest stage;
callers cannot skip ahead. Checkpoint references are confined to the project,
validated against project/pipeline/run identity, and must carry a legal status.
Human-gated stages pause as `awaiting_approval`, failed stages remain resumable,
and completed work orders cannot contradict their manifest-derived pointer.

Backlot exposes work-order read/claim/resume/heartbeat/advance/release routes.
The existing approval route now preserves checkpoint history, records the
explicit human decision, and resumes the active lease when one exists; a
released or expired lease remains approved but requires an agent to reclaim it.
