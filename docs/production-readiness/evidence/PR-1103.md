## Approved launch provider boundary

Under the approved Option A scope, the launch envelope is strictly restricted to local and source-footage workflows:

1. **`screen-demo`**: Local desktop/window capture + Remotion synthetic `TerminalScene`. No external cloud or paid AI APIs required.
2. **`talking-head`**: User-supplied input media + local Whisper transcription + local subtitle generator + local FFmpeg / Remotion audio and video compositing. No external paid AI APIs required.
3. **TTS**: `edge_tts` is configured as the local/zero-cost synthetic speech provider.

All commercial paid generative providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, ElevenLabs, Runway, Pika, Kling, Luma) are held in **Beta** or **Experimental** tiers and are disabled from default Wave 1 pipelines.
