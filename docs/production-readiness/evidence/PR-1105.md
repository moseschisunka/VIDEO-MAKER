`REL-05` requires executing and certifying security, recovery, and deployment rollback drills against the frozen candidate in a realistic production-like environment before entering canaries.

> [!WARNING]

They have not been executed on live deployed infrastructure. Furthermore, prerequisite `PR-10G` is BLOCKED. Therefore, `REL-05` cannot be credited as certified.
