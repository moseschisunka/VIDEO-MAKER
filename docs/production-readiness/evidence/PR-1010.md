# PR-1010 — Operator and incident runbooks

Status: **VERIFIED (documentation contract)**

Added [`docs/operations/RUNBOOKS.md`](../../operations/RUNBOOKS.md), a
fail-closed operator guide covering:

- pre-deploy and container/local deployment checks;
- bad-deploy rollback with immutable image/ref and state preservation;
- provider outage, quota, timeout, circuit, and material-fallback handling;
- stuck, abandoned, duplicate, cancellation, lease-reclaim, and restart flow;
- corrupt/partial/wrong artifact quarantine and current-run provenance checks;
- staged backup/restore and versioned migration;
- secret rotation, revocation, redaction, and privacy incidents;
- P0/P1 evidence capture, SLO response, and closure criteria.

Every procedure names its stop conditions, preserves durable records, and keeps
provider/runtime/voice/profile identity fail-closed. The document explicitly
states that production remains locked until `PR-11G`.
