## Purpose and boundary

This task classifies every pipeline into a proposed `launch`, `beta`, `experimental`, or
`test` lane. It does not certify any pipeline as production-ready. Certification remains
subject to the phase gates and the final `PR-11G` gate. The product must not advertise a
pipeline as production-ready merely because its manifest currently says `stability:
production`.

The classification was deliberately based on the live repository and capability baseline,
not on manifest filenames or declared stability alone. No provider, runtime, media type, or
fallback was silently substituted.

## Inventory method

- Loaded all `pipeline_defs/*.yaml` manifests through `lib.pipeline_loader.load_pipeline_readonly`.
- Inspected each manifest's category, declared stability, stage order, required/optional
  tools, human-approval defaults, and director-skill references.
- Compared those contracts with the `provider_menu_summary()` and runtime results recorded
  in [`PR-000`](PR-000.md).
- Kept `framework-smoke` explicitly test-only.
- Recorded `documentary-montage` as a release blocker because category `documentary` is
  rejected by `schemas/pipelines/pipeline_manifest.schema.json`.

## Recommended first-launch scope

### Wave 1 launch lane (recommended option A)

Expose only these two source-footage/local-runtime workflows after their relevant phase
gates and final release checks pass:

1. **`screen-demo`** — real capture for live app/browser surfaces and synthetic Remotion
   `TerminalScene` for predictable CLI/install/config demonstrations.
2. **`talking-head`** — user-supplied talking-head footage, local transcription/captions,
   audio preparation, and an explicitly selected certified composition runtime.

The launch lane does **not** include avatar generation, AI video generation, stock retrieval
as a hidden substitute for missing source media, or an automatic Remotion/HyperFrames swap.
If a selected runtime or provider is unavailable, the run must stop with a structured
blocker and preserve the decision log.

### Held lanes

- **Beta:** `cinematic`, `clip-factory`, `podcast-repurpose`, `hybrid`, `animation`, and
  `character-animation`.
- **Experimental:** `animated-explainer`, `avatar-spokesperson`, `localization-dub`, and
  `documentary-montage`.
- **Test only:** `framework-smoke`.

This is a staged product boundary, not a decision to abandon the held workflows. The
roadmap continues to harden all of them; a held lane can move forward only when its
manifest, provider envelope, runtime path, artifacts, approvals, and acceptance rows agree.

## Evidence driving the recommendation

The baseline in [`PR-000`](PR-000.md) records:

- 13 manifests discovered; 12 load and `documentary-montage` fails schema validation.
- FFmpeg and Remotion available; HyperFrames unavailable/not certified.
- `image_generation` `0/14`, `video_generation` `0/23`, `avatar` `0/4`,
  `clip_retrieval` `0/1`, `corpus_population` `0/1`, and `source_ingest` `0/1`.
- `tts` `1/11` with Edge TTS as the observed configured provider.
- Capability preflight is 104.3499 seconds cold and 29.2020 seconds warm; this is routed
  to `PR-307` and is not a reason to hide capability state.
- The full offline suite still has the known provider-catalog drift and Remotion
  `edit_decisions` `NameError` failures routed to `PR-300`/`PR-306`/`PR-309` and `PR-200`.

These facts make a narrow local/source launch the only honest first-launch recommendation
without claiming that generation, avatar, corpus, or HyperFrames workflows already work.

## Scope decision and resolution

The release owner was asked to select one of the following:

| Option | Decision | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **A — reliability-first (recommended)** | Launch only `screen-demo` and source-footage `talking-head`; keep all other lanes beta/experimental/test as listed above. | Smallest honest surface; allows the roadmap to certify a usable local path while the remaining workflows are hardened. |
| **B — source-footage expansion** | Also place `cinematic`, `clip-factory`, and/or `podcast-repurpose` in launch. | Broader value, but requires accepting additional multi-output, audio, and source-media risk and completing those gates before release. |
| **C — broad launch** | Advertise all non-test manifests at launch. | Not approvable from the current baseline; requires provider/runtime setup, manifest repair, and full phase certification first. |

Before the decision below, `PR-002` was `BLOCKED`; no production label, release candidate,
or `PR-0G` pass may be inferred from an inventory alone.

On 2026-09-02, the project release owner approved **Option A — reliability-first** as a
standing decision for this chat. The approved launch lane is therefore `screen-demo` and
source-footage `talking-head`; all other lanes retain the proposed beta, experimental, or
test classification above until their own evidence and release gates pass. This approval
does not certify either launch candidate and does not waive any P0/P1 finding.
