# PR-406 — Voice-rate script calibration

Status: **VERIFIED**

`validate_script_duration` counts authored narration words, applies the
declared voice rate and timing overhead, and rejects scripts whose natural
speech cannot fit a target contract. Content-led growth is explicit and
reported as a warning; no minimum-duration padding or hidden speed change is
used to claim a short-form fit.

Evidence: `tests/contracts/test_phase4_timing.py` overlong, content-led, and
word-budget cases.
